We finished Big Walk this week!!!! Amazing game, easily the best game I've played this year and one of my favorite co-op games ever. The final section was so cool and the ending was honestly so emotional, especially playing with like 10 people!! We need to go back and finish every puzzle, but it'll also be nice to take a bit of a break haha

I'm so glad a game like Big Walk exists, I know people love to make fun of "friendslop" games but I think games like this are sooooo amazing, non-competitive multiplayer games as a vehicle for both conversation, but also building a shared language and solving puzzles together. It so expertly balances the discussion of game mechanics with having natural pauses between them to have non-game related conversation, little moments where you can pause and just enjoy a sunset or train ride together.

The puzzle design itself was fantastic too, consistently utilizing collaborative solving and decision making, making people develop a unique vocabulary for the symbols, the sounds, whatever that gourd thing is. So many puzzles obfuscate information from each other or have information that's not clearly communicable so you need to create systems to tell your puzzle partners what lit up or what symbols appeared. It's so good!!!!!!! So many moments I keep thinking about. I know I know most of the puzzle solutions now, but I'd love to go back and replay it sometime (or I can join Mariah's game :3 )
