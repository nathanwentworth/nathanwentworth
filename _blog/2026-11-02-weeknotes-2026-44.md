I finally got around to finishing Silent Hill 3 in time for our work game club discussion about it. Really incredible game, though not without some frustrations! Completely unmatched visual aesthetic and auditory experience, easily one of the best looking PS2 games I've played, and one of the best looking games I've played in general. The lighting, texture work, and models all look fantastic and the sound design is unbeatable. I keep thinking about this specific area, the 3rd floor employee wing of the nightmare hospital, where the soundtrack is just this [deep sub-bass wobbling sound](https://youtu.be/UU_nKJZnyDs?t=12468), incredibly effective.

I kept thinking about how SH3 felt like the inverse of SH2 in a lot of ways. SH2 is all about James' internality and the town becomes a reflection of his inner demons, where SH3 feels more like the world is a reflection of what is thrust upon her and what she's forced to deal with. Almost every character you meet in SH2 are your equals in a way, they all have something they're struggling with and their town reflects that, but for Heather almost every person she meets is trying to use her or manipulate her in some way. The ending especially, so incredibly visceral the way she's being used. The level design feels more claustrophobic than SH2, there's no "open" areas until halfway through the game, and even then you're barely actually walking around it, instead being funneled back into tight hallways. When you're in the hospital, reading the notes left by Stanley Coleman, it feels even more like you're always being followed or watched by a menacing presence.

I played on easy action/easy puzzle which I kinda regret, I found it often a bit too easy, like I never died from combat and barely needed to use my healing items or ammo since monsters were few and far between and pretty easy to kill. Maybe I'll play it again sometime!! And if I play on normal puzzle then the puzzles will be different so I won't even have the solutions memorized which is nice.
