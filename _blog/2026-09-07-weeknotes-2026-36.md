Two weeks ago I mentioned my upcoming Utah trip to friend of friend Jessica who is a big climber/hiker, and she was like "two 10 mile hikes in two days when you don't hike? good luck!" so over the weekend I took it upon myself to try to go for a long walk around the city, but specifically focusing on hills to match the elevation gain of the hikes we're going to do (1,100ft). I didn't track the first few hills, but I'd estimate the total I did was around 600ft and my legs were killing me after. So I think she was right lmao!!! Definitely need to spend the next two weeks going for long, hilly walks after work to train myself a bit. When I went to Yosemite last year and we walked up to the vernal falls, that was close to 1,100 (but the top was closed, so probably closer to like 800 if I had to guess?). That didn't feel too bad, but I also had been walking a lot the previous few days so I had kinda warmed up. Vs this trip where I'm just going in cold after a normal work week and then sitting in a plane/car all day. Any day I'm not doing my hilly walks I should be playing DDR to get some stamina back too!!!

On that walk I dropped off two rolls of film I've shot over the last couple months so I'm excited to finally see those!!! It's at the point where I definitely don't remember a lot of the shots anymore (even if I usually take the same shot with my fuji anyway).
