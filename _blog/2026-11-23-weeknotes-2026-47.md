Continued my week of concerts with another week of concerts! And a movie and some ping pong and more Big Walk and Halo and other fun activities. Though as much as I love doing something literally every day, I'm excited for this next week with very few plans. Not even as like a "recharge" thing, just as an opportunity to not go out for a break.

Big Walk Monday was good again!! Getting really close, I think we have like six or seven gourds left for 100%? We've been doing a balance of Big puzzles that take a bit to complete as well as some of the easier ones. It's been really fun seeing the remixed puzzles near the end, but we're also kiiiiinda cheating on a couple of these. Like the ones where we Get It and the process just doesn't seem particularly fun. Which I'm totally okay with!!!! We did the vast majority of the game without outside communication or looking anything up so I'm fine with a little bit near the very end.

On Tuesday after work I walked to the local bookstore to pick up On the Calculation of Volume V (aka calcvol), and then loitered in Muji for a little bit (no jackets I wanted.....) and then walked over to the venue to see [caroline](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rAg3YuHkMHQ). Their album caroline 2 was one of my favorites last year and I was so curious how they would translate some of the more experimental stuff to a live performance. It was so cool!!! Like for Coldplay Cover they had each half of the band on different sides of the stage, turned off all of their mics, and then one of the members walked between each group for each half of the song (while you could still hear the non-amplified half still playing). I had intended on reading a bunch of calcvol while I was waiting for the bands to come on, but I ended up running into a friend and I chatted with her and her friends the whole time, so that worked out well I think!

It's funny to think of when I would hang out with Steph a couple years ago and she's always run into people she knew when going places and I was so jealous of that, and now I feel like I'm finally getting to that point where I just run into people I know when I go out to do things.

Wednesday I met up with Snow and Maham to play ping pong again, always fun!!! Afterwards I met up with two different coworkers to see Raise the Red Lantern at the local theater in the new 4K restoration, which looked great. I think I had watched it in high school in a history class but I definitely did not appreciate it at the time, so I'm glad I watched it again! Love Zhang Yimou and Gong Li

Back to the concerts, Wednesday I saw Frost Children (SOOOOOO FUN) and Thursday I saw Sara Landry (also fun!!) Frost Children I saw pretty much exactly a year ago which is funny, their set was pretty different, in that they only played new stuff outside of Fox Bop and they played a few remixed versions of songs. Sara Landry was good, crowd was kinda awful though. As Jonah put it, the people who were good were the ones who were clearly there for a "concert" experience, and the ones who were annoying were the ones who were just there for a night of going out. Like I will never understand why groups of people push into the crowd where there's barely space and then just stand there talking to each other????? If you want to chat go to the bar or something not in the middle of the crowd!!

Felt very European and calcvol-like honestly...... well I guess the first calcvol when they weren't worried about Consumption.

After reading more calcvol I went to a friendsgiving party (I made an irish soda bread, it stuck to the bottom of the dish so it was messed up but still tasted yummy!) and it was fun!! Everyone is such a great cook 🙂‍↕️ Sunday we had some people over to play Halo again which is always fun!!!! yayyyyyyy

A sad one, really, it's tough seeing Tara lose a lot of the relationships she's built up over the last ~3500 days, and even tougher seeing how the days get further and further apart.
