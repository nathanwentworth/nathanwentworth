Finally not sick anymore :'))))) !!!! Monday I went to urgent care and they put me on antibiotics which seem to have helped since by the end of the week I was feeling mostly better!! yay yippee

Monday we were back playing Big Walk, trying to clean up the rest of the puzzles and get 100% completion. We played for around two hours, the first hour was struggling even just getting a puzzle set up, with two people getting lost and it taking a while for them to get back. So after a bunch of frustrations some people quit and it was down to just four of us, but we kept going and in the second hour flew through three puzzles. Basically speedran designing communication systems for those since the late-game puzzles are hard-mode variants of puzzles that we'd seen earlier so we kinda got the idea. While I love playing with a big group it was also nice just having four people who were pretty locked in on figuring them out so there was basically zero gaps in communication. Though hopefully we can get more of a group next time!!!!

Friday Sean and I went to Ethan's to re-watch Godzilla Minus One (Sean's first watch, my third), sooooooooo so good as always. Crazy how much his setup sold me on HDR and surround sound, even though I mostly watch movies on my computer (which Ethan will be mad about again when he reads this lol sorry).
