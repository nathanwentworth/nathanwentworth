Finally got around to finishing The Left Hand of Darkness, ended up really enjoying it! The first half is the kind of sci-fi/fantasy I don't like, which is very "political" in that it's all about like, government relations and trade negotiations and land disputes and broad societal things that I just find it hard to connect with, but the second half has a shift into a much more personal story just following the two main characters and I really loved it from there onward. Lots of interesting things to think about in it, kinda crazy to think it was written in the 60s with how it discusses gender and just the way it was written (though I feel like if it was written now things would be pretty different, like there's no usage of singular "they" in here). Definitely want to read more of her novels now, maybe Earthsea next??? After I read some more books that I already own, and calcvol 5 comes out in a month..........
