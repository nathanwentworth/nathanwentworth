Waow I went to four concerts this week!!! Also an art market! And rewatched Dune!

Continuing my concert marathon starting with Saosin last week, I saw Ryan Hemsworth/George Clanton on Monday. I was pretty much entirely there for Ryan so I bought his CD and a t-shirt and chatted with him for a bit. His set was great obv but I do wish it was longer!!! I ended up leaving like 20 minutes into the headliner since I really just wanted to go to sleep lol.

Tuesday I saw Geese with Gabby and Chloe, really great show! Really cool seeing a show where almost every song was a little different than the studio recording. Like they'd play with the tempos or structures in interesting ways. Also they did not say a single thing to the crowd the entire show lmao, no encore no "hey how's everyone doing", just walking on stage and playing for 90 minutes with a single "Boston, Massachusetts!!!!!" in the middle of one of the songs and that's it. Powerful.

Wednesday I saw Brutalismus 3000 which was incredibly fun, easily one of the best shows I've been to this year??? Like the most fun EDM set along with Ninajirachi, but B3K might even win out for just how non-stop high energy the whole thing was, like three great opener DJs who all transitioned into each other so by the time that they went on the energy was already so high. And I just love hardstyle lmao. I went with Jonah and he was directly in the middle of the pit and I had to get out of there when B3K went on, just way too many people, so I stood off to the side by the bar and was by this group of EDM festival guys and they were losing it when [this came on](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=COOC4F_uJFo)

Sunday I saw Trophy Wife with Gabby and Chloe which was really fun!! Two local bands opened and they were both great. We went there directly from the Boston Art Book Fair which was great as always. It definitely feels like it's been going further towards a MICE-style "cute illustration and stickers" type fair which is interesting, I feel like in earlier years it was a lot more expensive photography/graphic design/etc books. I'm not really complaining, I love cute prints and stuff, but it is interesting to see!!

{% include img.html url='small/IMG_1177.jpeg' lg='large/IMG_1177.jpeg' alt='' %}

I felt like I could justify buying a bunch of art on Sunday since Saturday I finally spent the time to do an art refresh in my room, replacing most of the stuff above my desk and hanging up prints that have been framed for almost a year now. Ever since I stayed with Jane in Seattle I've been inspired by her roommate's wall above their desk, it felt very delicate and collage-like, a mixture of prints and objects and textures, so I wanted to do something more like that!! So I went with a mix of framed prints, unframed prints, and objects and I'm quite pleased with it n_n I love buying art but I do always struggle what to really do with it. Books are more straightforward since I can kinda just put them on a shelf and easily look through them, but prints if they're not on a wall I just kinda put them in a bin or a pile waiting to be hung up which isn't my favorite.
